# Insurance Claims & Fraud Risk Intelligence

## Project Overview
An insurance analytics project designed to analyse claims, monitor financial exposure, identify potential fraud-risk patterns, and evaluate payment performance using Python, Statistics and Power BI.

## Business Questions
- What is the total number and value of insurance claims?
- Which claim types contribute the highest financial losses?
- What percentage of claims are flagged for potential fraud risk?
- Which regions have the highest claim frequency?
- How does previous claim history relate to claim amounts?
- What are the payment and investigation patterns?

## Tools & Technologies
- Python (Pandas, NumPy)
- Microsoft Excel
- Statistics & Exploratory Data Analysis
- Power BI & DAX

## Dashboard Pages

**1. Executive Overview**
- Total Claims and Claim Amount

**2. Fraud & Risk Analysis**

- Previous Claims Analysis

**3. Financial & Claims Analysis**
- Total Claim Amount

- Average Claim Amount

- Payment Status Distribution

## Dataset
The project includes six related datasets:
- Customers
- Policies
- Claims
- Payments
- Investigations
- Claim Types

## Key Objective
To transform insurance data into meaningful business insights, improve claims monitoring, highlight potential risk patterns, and support data-driven decision-making.

## Important Note
The dataset is for analytical practice. Fraud flags indicate potential risk and do not represent confirmed fraud cases.

Insurance | Financial Services | Business Intelligence
